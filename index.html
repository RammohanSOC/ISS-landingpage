Questions we hear first

How does buying Microsoft 365 licences through you work?

We supply licences as a Microsoft Cloud Solution Provider (CSP). You keep your existing Microsoft 365 tenant and data. We handle billing, licence changes and support, and review assignments regularly so you are not paying for unused seats.

How is pricing structured?

Licences are billed per user on the plan and term you choose. Assessments, red team engagements and migrations are scoped and quoted as projects. Managed services are priced per user, device or environment. You receive a written quote before any work starts.

What does a Red Team engagement cover?

Scope is agreed in writing before we begin. It can include external and internal networks, web and mobile applications, cloud environments and phishing or social engineering. You receive a report with findings ranked by risk, how each was exploited, and how to fix it. A retest can confirm the fixes.

Can you help us meet compliance requirements?

We map findings and controls to the frameworks you are working towards, such as ISO 27001 or India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, and help close the gaps. Certification itself is issued by an accredited auditor, not by us.

What support and response times do you offer?

Response and resolution times are agreed with you in writing before a service starts, based on severity. Ask us about round-the-clock coverage for managed security monitoring.

Do we have to replace the tools we already use?

No. The assessment starts with what you run today. We recommend changes only where there is a clear security gap or a cost saving.
